### 1. Survivorship: bhavcopy-based point-in-time reconstruction — Evidence: **Proven** *(serves FAIL #2; concrete unblock for PARKED B1)*
- **What:** A 2026 study of India's **NIFTY Smallcap 250 (2016–2025, 1,437 stocks)** quantifies the bias and gives a method: survivor-only backtests **overstate annual return by 4.94 pp (23.3%)** and **Sharpe by 0.097 (9.1%)**; index turnover **82.5%** (delisted 16.1% / graduated 33.1% / demoted 33.2%). Correction = a **price-volume ranking over daily *bhavcopy* (which includes delisted securities)** to reconstruct historical membership — **100% accuracy for current, ~85–90% historically**.
- **Why it matters:** Our `event_outcomes` is **96.6% dividend + ~100% current-listed survivors** — exactly this bias. The audit's PARKED **B1** waits to *accrue* `nse500_membership_history` going forward; this method lets you reconstruct it **backwards from free NSE bhavcopy** — a concrete unblock path, not a re-proposal.
- **What you'd consider:** A read-only sidecar that rebuilds point-in-time NSE membership from bhavcopy, then re-scores historical alpha on the survivor-free set. *(Direction only — not a code change.)*
- **Catch / risk:** ~85–90% historical accuracy (not perfect); bhavcopy parsing is fiddly; small-cap study — magnitude on large-caps is likely smaller. Recent (2026), so lightly peer-reviewed.
- **Source:** [arXiv 2603.19380 — Survivorship Bias in India's NIFTY Smallcap 250](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19380)

### 2. Two-AI consensus: Cohen's kappa — Evidence: **Proven** *(closes auditor blind-spot "two-AI consensus NOT MEASURED")*
- **What:** Agreement between two raters, **chance-corrected**, is a settled statistic — **Cohen's κ** (κ<0.4 poor, 0.4–0.6 moderate, 0.6–0.8 substantial, >0.8 excellent).
- **Why it matters:** The audit watches whether one model silently dominates but **doesn't measure consensus quality**. κ over Haiku-vs-DeepSeek BUY/SELL/SKIP (from the `agent_disagreements` table) turns that WATCH into a number — and flags if the "two-AI" system has quietly collapsed to one.
- **What you'd consider:** A read-only metric computing κ on stored decisions, reported alongside the AI-SL canary.
- **Catch / risk:** κ is **unstable on tiny samples** — with so few live trades it needs a confidence interval or a minimum-n gate before it means anything (ties to the audit's LOW-CONFIDENCE maturity gate).
- **Source:** [BMC Med. Res. Methodology — agreement coefficients for nominal data](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12874-016-0200-9)
